    How to use Neuroatlas

    • Hover or tab across the diagram to name a structure; click it to read what it does, what happens when it's damaged, and one thing worth remembering.
    • Run an action from the left. A packet of activity travels the actual route the signal takes, pausing at each relay so you can read what that structure contributes.
    • Functional areas (motor strip, Broca's, Wernicke's, V1…) sit on top of the lobes — toggle them on at the bottom left.
    • Quiz me turns the diagram into a test: read the function, click the structure.

    Anatomy is schematic — a teaching diagram, not a scan. Deep structures are drawn as if the hemisphere were cut down the middle.
